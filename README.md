# Vulcanizing Academy

A self-study PWA for the tyre and vulcanizing trade: 3 semesters, 19 lessons, 219 questions with explanations, 27 diagrams, a roadside quick guide, review of missed questions, semester tests, a 30-question final exam and a certificate.

## Files
- `index.html`: the app.
- `content.js`: lessons (notes, in-depth notes, diagrams and the first question set).
- `questions.js`: extra questions per lesson and the semester tests.
- `vextra.js`: explanations for every question (matched by question text), learning outcomes for every lesson, extra diagrams and the standards page text.
- `sw.js`: offline cache. Change the cache name (`vulc-vN`) whenever any file changes. Files are re-downloaded fresh on install.

## Notes
- The final exam picks one question from every lesson and fills to 30 at random.
- The course follows standard tyre repair and servicing practice. It is not accredited or approved by any body, and no published NVTI or COTVET standard for vulcanizing was found. Check local rules and the equipment maker's data.
